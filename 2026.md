# Halloween 2026

2026 sees the introduction of the new Mark 3 boxes which are entirely
3D printed and more versatile than both the previous Mark 1 and Mark 2
boxes as they screw together in sections. For the 2026 display, we will
be adding at least 3 boxes and (if time allows) replacing the 3 Mark 1
boxes with Mark 3 boxes. The current Mark 3 boxes are all battery powered
for ease of installation and use. A future enhancement is to support USB
power.

The Mark 3 boxes also come with the entirely new software stack that is
more efficient so only a single Pico is required per box rather than the
2 Picos required in the Mark 1 and 2 boxes. We have also prototyped using
Pico 2 and ESP32-S3 boards that are both more powerful and have
significantly more RAM available. The more powerful devices, improved
software stack and improved boxes have opened up a lot of options for
future enhancements to the display.

The new software stack and boxes have taken a significant amount of time
to prepare so  there are no major changes to the layout of display, which
remains identical in terms of overall to the 2025 display. We've just
added more stuff. For a description of how the installation area is
arranged and split into "zones", please refer to [layout](./layout.md).
